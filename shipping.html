We hand-finish every apron, so we'd rather tell you honestly how long things take than promise a date we can't keep. Here's exactly how shipping works at Custom Leather Apron.

Processing time

Processing time is the time it takes us to prepare your apron before it's handed to the carrier. Orders are processed Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

  • Aprons without personalization: 1–3 business days.
  • Personalized or engraved aprons: 3–5 business days after you approve your engraving proof.
  • Team and bulk orders: we'll confirm a production timeline with your quote.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM (CST) or on weekends begin processing the next business day. During peak seasons such as the holidays, processing can take a little longer — we'll always email you if your order is delayed.

International orders

We ship to most countries worldwide. International orders may be subject to import duties, taxes and customs fees charged by the destination country. These charges are not included in our prices or shipping costs and are the responsibility of the recipient.

Customs policies vary widely from country to country, so we recommend checking with your local customs office before ordering. Parcels held or delayed by customs are outside our control, but we'll do everything we can to help.

Order tracking

As soon as your order ships, we'll email you a shipping confirmation with a tracking number and a link to follow your parcel. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show its first update.

Address changes

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you need to change it, email us at sales@customleatherapron.com as soon as possible — we can update it any time before your order ships. Once a parcel is with the carrier we can't redirect it, and we aren't responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address provided at checkout.

Lost, late or damaged parcels

If your tracking hasn't updated for 7 business days, your parcel shows as delivered but hasn't arrived, or your apron arrives damaged, contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date. Please include your order number and, for damaged items, a photo of the apron and the packaging.

We'll open a claim with the carrier and, where the parcel is confirmed lost or damaged in transit, send a replacement or issue a full refund at no extra cost to you.
