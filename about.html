Why We Make Leather Aprons

Every trade has its uniform. For chefs, barbers, woodworkers, artists and pitmasters, it's the apron — the one piece of kit that's there for every shift, every cut and every late night. We think it deserves to be built like a tool, not sold like a T-shirt.

So we start with full-grain leather, the strongest layer of the hide. We stitch it with waxed thread, reinforce the corners with rivets, fit cross-back straps that are kind to your neck, and finish everything in solid brass. Then we engrave your name or logo on the chest — because the best tools are the ones that are unmistakably yours.
