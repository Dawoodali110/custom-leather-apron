Questions Before You Write?

How do I send you my logo?+

Attach it to the form below or include it in your email. PNG, JPG, SVG or PDF all work — a high-resolution file or a vector gives the cleanest engraving. We'll send a proof for your approval before anything is engraved.

Can I order aprons for my whole team?+

Yes. Restaurants, barbershops, cafés, breweries and workshops can order matching aprons with their logo on every piece, and you can mix colours and styles in one order. Choose "Team / bulk order" above and tell us roughly how many you need.

Do you ship worldwide?+

Yes, we ship worldwide, and shipping is free on orders over $99. Delivery times depend on your location and whether your apron is personalized — ask us for an estimate before you order.

Can you help me choose the right apron?+

Absolutely. Tell us your trade, the tools you carry and how you like to work, and we'll point you to the style and colour that fits best.

What does the warranty cover?+

Every apron comes with a 1-year craftsmanship warranty. If a stitch, strap or rivet fails through normal use, contact us with a photo and your order details and we'll make it right.
