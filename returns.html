We want you to love your apron for years. If something isn't right, here's exactly how returns, refunds and our warranty work.

Return eligibility

You can return any non-personalized apron within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange, as long as it is:

  • Unused and unworn, with no signs of wear, stains, odours or damage;
  • In its original condition, with any tags and original packaging;
  • Accompanied by your order number or proof of purchase.

Full-grain leather is a natural material, so small variations in colour, grain and markings are part of its character and are not considered defects.

Personalized items

Aprons engraved with a name, logo, initials or other custom text are made specifically for you, so they cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged, defective or with an engraving error on our part.

To help you get it right, we send a digital proof of your engraving before production. Please check spelling, spacing and logo placement carefully, once you approve the proof, we engrave exactly what you approved.

How to return an item

  1. Contact us at sales@customleatherapron.com within 30 days of delivery with your order number and the item you'd like to return.
  2. Receive your return instructions. We'll reply with the return address and next steps. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.
  3. Pack and ship the apron securely in its original packaging, using a trackable shipping service.

Return shipping costs are paid by the customer, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or incorrect. Original shipping charges are non-refundable.

Refunds

Once your return arrives, we'll inspect it and email you to confirm whether your refund has been approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take a few extra days for the credit to appear on your statement.

If your refund hasn't appeared after 10 business days, please check with your bank first, then contact us.

Exchanges

Want a different colour or style? The fastest way to exchange a non-personalized apron is to return it for a refund and place a new order for the item you want. If you'd prefer, contact us and we'll arrange a direct exchange, subject to availability.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your apron arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, including an engraving that doesn't match your approved proof, contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number and photos. We'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund, including shipping costs, and cover the cost of any return.

Craftsmanship warranty

Every Custom Leather Apron apron is covered by a 1 year craftsmanship warranty from the date of delivery. If a stitch comes loose, a rivet fails, or hardware breaks through normal use, we'll repair or replace the apron free of charge.

The warranty doesn't cover normal wear and patina, damage from accidents, misuse, cuts, burns, chemicals, machine washing, or improper care. To make a claim, email us with your order number, a description of the issue and photos.
