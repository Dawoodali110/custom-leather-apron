How to Choose the Right Leather Apron

For the kitchen

Choose a full-bib chef apron with deep utensil pockets, a knife sleeve and a towel ring. Leather handles oil spatter and oven heat, and wipes clean between services.

For the barbershop

Look for smooth black leather that hair brushes straight off, with slim sleeves for clippers and trimmers and dedicated shear and comb pockets.

For the workshop

A woodworking apron needs tool pockets, a hammer loop and a pencil holder — plus reinforced corners that stand up to sawdust, glue and sharp edges.

For the studio

Painters and potters need tall brush pockets, a palette-knife slot and a towel ring. Leather wipes clean and wears every splash as character.

For the grill

A BBQ apron should take heat and flare-ups in its stride, with pockets for a spatula, fork, brush and thermometer, and a sleeve for the sauce.

Make it personal

Every apron includes a chest patch we engrave with your logo, name or initials for $15 — the finishing touch for teams and gifts alike.
