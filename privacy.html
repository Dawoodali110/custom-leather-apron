Introduction

Custom Leather Apron ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit our website, contact us or place an order, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Contact details — your name, email address and, if you choose to provide it, your phone number, when you contact us, request a quote or subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Order information — your billing and shipping address, items purchased and personalization details such as names, initials or logos you ask us to engrave.
  • Files you send us — logos or reference images you share for engraving.
  • Messages — anything you write to us by email or through our contact form.

Payment information is entered directly with our payment processor and is never stored on our servers. We do not see or keep your full card number.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider and the services we use may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and the date and time of your visit.

How we use your information

  • To process, personalize, ship and support your orders;
  • To send engraving proofs, order confirmations, shipping updates and replies to your messages;
  • To prepare quotes for team and bulk orders;
  • To send our newsletter and offers, only if you've subscribed — you can unsubscribe at any time;
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website secure;
  • To improve our products, website and customer service;
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share personal information only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, and only as much as they need to do their job:

  • Payment processors to securely process payments;
  • Shipping carriers to deliver your order;
  • Email and hosting providers to operate our website and communicate with you;
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect our rights, customers or the public.

These providers are required to keep your information confidential and use it only for the services they provide to us.

Cookies & local storage

Our website uses your browser's local storage to remember the items in your shopping cart. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us until you place an order. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files.

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, such as Google Analytics or Google Ads, they may use cookies to understand how visitors use our site and to measure the effectiveness of our ads. We'll update this policy when we do. You can block or delete cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings.

Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to fulfil your order, provide warranty support and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. Messages and files you send us are kept only as long as needed to respond and complete your request.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections across our website. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information;
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information;
  • Withdraw consent, such as unsubscribing from marketing emails;
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format.

Residents of California, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom have specific rights under the CCPA/CPRA and the GDPR. To exercise any of these rights, email sales@customleatherapron.com. We'll respond within the time required by law and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we take steps to make sure your information remains protected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.
