How personalization works

1. Choose and preview

Pick your leather colour and type the text for the chest patch, or upload a logo file. The preview on this page gives you a feel for size and colour.

2. Approve the proof

After you order we prepare the engraving artwork and email a proof. We only engrave once you reply to approve it, and you can ask for changes first.

3. Made and shipped

Your apron is cut, stitched and engraved, then shipped to you. Shipping is free on orders over $99.
